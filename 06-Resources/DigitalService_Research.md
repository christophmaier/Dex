## Political Context (2025–2026)

- **New BMDS ministry** created after September 2025 elections — Germany's first dedicated digital ministry
- **Minister:** Karsten Wildberger (CDU)
- **Budget:** €4B in 2025 funding (€2.9B broadband, €263M admin digitalization)
- This gives DigitalService a direct ministerial home with real mandate — inflection point for the org

---

## Challenges & Criticisms

- Bureaucratic culture resistant to agile methods
- **Digitalcheck adoption issues:** 46% lack examples/templates, 40% poor process integration, 32% late awareness — used at end of legislative process, not beginning
- Security-over-usability in procurement
- Funding uncertainty for individual projects
- High demand exceeds team capacity
- Classic IT procurement bodies (FITKO, ITZBund) operate on contractor/hierarchical models that compete culturally

---

## Their Approach vs. Classic IT Procurement

| | DigitalService | FITKO / ITZBund |
|--|--------------|----------------|
| Method | Agile, in-house, user-centered | Hierarchical, external contractors |
| Goal | Digital sovereignty, no vendor lock-in | Shared IT standards/infrastructure |
| Critique | Needs proactive project selection | Vendor dependencies, implementation gaps |
